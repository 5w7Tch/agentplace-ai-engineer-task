# Edumentors — public facts (source of truth for the agent)

Collected 2026-11-04 from the public website only. Each fact lists its source page.

## What it is
- One-to-one **online** tutoring platform for pupils aged **4 to 19**. Parents find verified tutors and book through an online booking system. — /faq/parents
- All tutoring is online; sessions run on **Google Meet**, link auto-generated per booking (in session details and the tutor group chat). No face-to-face tutoring. — /pricing FAQ, /faq/parents
- Tutors ("Super-Tutors") are current students or very recent graduates of top UK universities (Oxford, Cambridge, Warwick, Imperial, KCL and other Russell Group). — /faq/parents, /
- Vetting: one-to-one interview by education experts (subject knowledge, communication, tutoring approach), academic achievements and certifications verified, identity verification. — /faq/parents, /lp/find-a-tutor

## Levels and subjects
- Levels: Early Years, KS1, KS2, 11+, KS3, GCSE, IGCSE, National 4&5, A-Level, IB. — footer, /find-tutors
- Subjects include Maths, Further Maths, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Combined Science, English Language, English Literature, History, Geography, Computer Science, Economics, Business Studies, Psychology, Law, Politics, Philosophy, Sociology, Statistics, Accountancy, Music, Art and Design, Design & Technology, ICT, C++, Creative Writing, Religious Studies, Media Studies, Sports Science, Health and Wellbeing, and languages (French, Spanish, German, Italian, Portuguese, Polish, Russian, Greek, Hebrew, Arabic, Chinese, Japanese, Latin). 11+ also has Verbal and Non-Verbal Reasoning. — nav menu

## Free trial
- Every tutor offers a **free 15-minute trial / introduction** session. No payment details needed to book it. — /faq/parents, /lp/find-a-tutor, /pricing FAQ

## Prices (tutors set their own)
- Each tutor sets their own price; it is shown on the tutor's profile. — /pricing FAQ
- Price tiers on /pricing: **New Tutors £20–30**, **Proven Performers £30–40** (50+ sessions completed), **Top Tutors £40+**.
- The platform adds a commission on top, which falls as more sessions are booked. Bulk bookings paid upfront get bigger discounts (example slider bands: 5–14, 15–29, 30–49, 50+ sessions). — /about-us FAQ, /pricing
- No hidden fees, subscriptions or added extras; all costs are shown inside the platform. — /pricing
- **Discount:** 20% off the first booking for **NHS members**. — /pricing FAQ
- Conflicting public price statements: "Tutoring can cost anywhere from £15 to £75" (/pricing FAQ), "From only £29.00/session" (/pricing hero), "sessions starting from £24" (/lp/find-a-tutor). All three are on the site.
- Pricing FAQ: "Refund eligibility depends on when you cancel. If a tutor misses a session, you are entitled to a refund or a reschedule." It points to the cancellation policy in the Terms. — /pricing FAQ

## Payment
- Two options: (1) pay all sessions upfront and get a bulk discount, or (2) add a payment method and be charged automatically **24 hours before each session** (pay-as-you-go, like a Direct Debit). — /faq/parents
- Payments via **Stripe**; card details are never entered on the Edumentors site. Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Apple Pay and Google Pay accepted; **Klarna** for instalments. — /faq/parents
- Mobile app (iOS/Android) for browsing tutors, booking, and managing sessions and payments. — /, /pricing FAQ

## Rescheduling, cancellation, refunds (Terms, rev. 10 Aug 2026)
- Reschedule free if requested **at least 24 hours** before the start; late reschedules may incur a fee. Request in the platform; tutor approves. Not charged twice. — /pricing FAQ, /faq/parents
- Parent cancels **more than 24h** before: pays only the platform fee for that session. **Less than 24h**: full session price. Can't cancel after the session has started. — /terms
- If a cancelled booking had a bulk discount, the discount on completed sessions may be clawed back. — /terms
- Tutor cancels: parent not charged; if already charged, Edumentors finds a substitute tutor or refunds 100% within **5 business days**. — /terms
- Completed sessions are not refundable; disputes are investigated case by case. — /terms
- Not happy with the tutor: Edumentors replaces the tutor at no extra cost. — /faq/parents
