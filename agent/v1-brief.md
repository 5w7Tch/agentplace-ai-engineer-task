# v1 — baseline build

v1 is what a typical business owner gets from Agentplace on day one: a one-paragraph brief sent to the AI Builder, with no hand-written instructions or knowledge. Builder model and agent model: GPT-6 Luna (free).

Brief sent to the Builder (2026-11-04):

> Build an agent for edumentors.co.uk. Edumentors is a UK online tutoring platform that connects parents with vetted tutors from top UK universities (Oxford, Cambridge, Imperial and others) for one-to-one online lessons for pupils aged 4-19, from KS1 and 11+ to GCSE, A-Level and IB. The agent should answer parents' questions about how tutoring, pricing and payments work, help them work out what kind of tutor their child needs, and guide them to book a free trial session. Use our website for the details.

The instructions and knowledge the Builder generated from this brief are saved next to this file as `v1-generated-*` once the build finishes.
